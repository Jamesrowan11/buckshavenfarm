Our Farm

Quiet country, serious horsemanship

Bucks Haven Farm is a small, multi-discipline horse farm tucked into the rolling countryside of Highland, Maryland, right on the Montgomery–Howard county line. We keep the farm intentionally small so every horse is known by name — and every boarder feels like family.

Whether your passion is dressage, eventing, hunter/jumper, western, or simply an unhurried hack down a shaded trail, you and your horse are welcome here. Our boarders enjoy quality daily care, honest communication, and a barn culture built on respect for the horse.

  • Attentive daily care from an experienced barn team
  • Individualized feed & turnout programs
  • Direct access to Schooley Mill Park's equestrian trails
  • A friendly, drama-free boarding community

Boarding

Full-care boarding, done right

Every horse at Bucks Haven receives thoughtful, consistent care — the kind you'd give if you lived at the barn yourself.

Daily Care

Quality hay and grain tailored to each horse, stalls cleaned daily, fresh water always, blanketing and fly care with the seasons.

Turnout Program

Generous daily turnout in small, compatible groups — with all-weather paddocks so horses still get out when the fields are resting.

Facilities

Built for year-round riding

Indoor Arena

Ride through every season. Our indoor arena keeps lessons, training rides, and winter schooling on schedule regardless of the weather.

Arena Barn

Matted stalls steps from the indoor — convenient for lesson horses, layups, and riders who like to tack up close to the action.

Log Barn

Our classic log barn offers a quiet, traditional setting with the character only a Maryland farm can have.

Outdoor Ring

A spacious outdoor ring for flatwork, jumping, and fair-weather lessons under open sky.

All-Weather Turnout

Mud-season insurance: all-weather paddocks mean turnout continues even when Maryland weather doesn't cooperate.

Trail Access

Hack straight off the property to miles of maintained equestrian trails, including the Schooley Mill Park network.

Lessons & Trail Riding
